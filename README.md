 Bank Customer Churn Prediction

Project Overview

Bank Customer Churn Prediction is a machine learning and data analytics project designed to identify customers who are likely to leave the bank. Customer churn is a critical issue in the banking industry because losing existing customers can significantly impact revenue and profitability.

This project analyzes customer demographic and financial information to discover patterns that contribute to customer attrition and helps banks develop effective customer retention strategies.


Problem Statement

Banks face challenges in retaining customers due to increasing competition and changing customer preferences. Identifying customers who are likely to leave enables banks to take proactive measures and improve customer satisfaction.

The objective of this project is to predict customer churn using historical customer data and provide insights into the factors affecting customer retention.


Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and banking behavior.
* Identify factors influencing customer churn.
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
* Build a predictive model for churn classification.
* Visualize customer trends and churn patterns.
* Support decision-making through data-driven insights.

 Key Findings

1. Older customers are more likely to leave the bank.
2. Active members have lower churn rates.
3. Customers with higher account balances show varying churn behavior.
4. Geography significantly affects customer retention.
5. Customers using multiple bank products are less likely to churn.
6. Credit score influences customer loyalty.
7. Inactive customers contribute to higher churn rates.
8. Customer engagement improves retention.
9. Certain customer segments require targeted retention strategies.
10. Predictive analytics can help reduce customer loss.
